Mission Briefing

Project Overview

  • Primary Goal: Assist astronauts on long-duration missions with medical advice and diagnosis.
  • Availability: Provides medical support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
  • Machine Learning: System learns from cases and is trained to provide solutions for various medical scenarios.
  • Conclusion: Demonstrates the potential of AI to improve medical assistance in isolated environments.

Problem Statement

  • Lack of medical professionals on board long-duration missions.
  • Communication blackouts with Earth lasting up to 2 weeks.
  • Real-time consultation is impractical due to transmission delays.
  • Extreme isolation creates high risk for medical emergencies.

Key Challenges

  • Ensuring high medical accuracy and safety.
  • Secure handling of sensitive crew health data.
  • Creating a user-friendly interface for high-stress settings.

Technology & Constraints

Core Functions

  • Data Collection: Continuous health tracking via biometric and environmental sensors.
  • Recommendation Engine: Personalized advice based on sensor data and previous history.
  • Machine Learning: Advanced AI processing of health trends and anomalies.
  • Privacy: All crew data is strictly private and locally encrypted.
  • Multi-Modal UI: Interactive interface supporting keyboard, audio, and real-time feeds.

System Constraints

  • Optimization for limited local computing power.
  • Ensuring software compatibility across different platforms.
  • Strict adherence to data privacy and security protocols.

Environmental Constraints

  • Connectivity: System must function entirely without internet access.
  • Atmosphere: Support for noisy or distracting spacecraft settings.
  • Diversity: Adaptable for different users and mission conditions.
