ASTRO AID

An AI Medical Assistant for Deep Space

Our main goal is to assist Astronauts on long-duration missions to obtain critical medical diagnosis and advice.

  • Always Available: Provides medical guidance 7 days a week, 24 hours a day.
  • Evolving Intelligence: Our AI learns from cases and is trained to provide detailed solutions for most medical scenarios.
  • Impact: Demonstrating the power of AI to bridge healthcare gaps between Earth and the stars.

The Mission

The Problem

Astronauts face extreme isolation and communication delays:

  • No doctors on board for deep space transit.
  • Communication blackouts lasting up to 2 weeks.
  • Real-time consultation with Earth is often impractical.

Challenges

  • Ensuring 100% medical accuracy and safety.
  • Collecting and handling sensitive biometric data.
  • Developing a user-friendly interface for high-stress environments.

Technology & Constraints

Core Functions

  • Biometric Monitoring: Continuous health tracking using wearable and environmental sensors.
  • Personalized Engine: Recommendations based on current issues, sensor data, and medical history.
  • Privacy: All crew data is handled with total encryption and privacy.
  • Processing: High-level Machine Learning and AI processing.
  • Multi-Model UI: Interaction via keyboard, audio, and real-time data feeds.

System Constraints

  • Limited computing power available on-station.
  • Platform-wide software compatibility.
  • Strict data privacy requirements.

Environmental Constraints

  • No Internet Access: System must function entirely offline.
  • Noisy/distracting spacecraft environments.
  • Support for multiple unique crew members.
