Our Objective

Space travel presents unique challenges to the human body. As we push towards Mars and beyond, astronauts need medical independence.


Astro Aid is an integrated hardware and software solution developed by high school students for the NASA HUNCH program. Our goal is to create a lightweight, intuitive diagnostic tool that assists crew members in identifying medical anomalies without immediate ground support.

System Capabilities

Vitals Monitoring

Real-time tracking of heart rate, oxygen saturation, and blood pressure using non-invasive sensors adapted for microgravity environments.

AI Analysis

On-board machine learning algorithms compare astronaut data against baseline health metrics to predict potential health risks before they become critical.

Crew Independence

Designed for deep space missions with significant communication delays, allowing the crew to make informed medical decisions autonomously.
